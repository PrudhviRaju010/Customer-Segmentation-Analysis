# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Customer Segmentation Analysis Dashboard**
built using **Power BI, SQL, and Excel** for a retail business with multiple
customers across different value segments.

The goal of this project is to classify customers based on their purchasing
behavior, analyze their contribution to overall sales and profit, and provide
actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention and revenue growth.

---

## Objectives

-  Classify customers into meaningful segments based on
  total sales and profit contribution
- Identify High Value customers who drive the most
  revenue and profitability for the business
- Analyze which customer segments are underperforming
  or being incorrectly categorized
- Compare profit margins across customer groups to
  understand true business value of each segment
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve
  customer retention and revenue growth strategy
- Support business decision-making through interactive
  visualizations and clear reporting
---

## Features

- **Customer Segmentation** — Customers classified into
  High Value, Medium Value, and Low Value groups based
  on sales and profit data
- **Segment-wise Sales Analysis** — Visual comparison of
  total sales contribution by each customer segment
- **Profit Analysis by Segment** — Deep dive into profit
  margins and profitability across all 3 segments
- **Individual Customer Drill-down** — Customer-level view
  of sales (₹3,96,340 – ₹4,51,006) and profit performance
- **Pivot Analysis in Excel** — Summary tables showing
  segment-wise sales (₹8,93,243 / ₹4,13,194 / ₹12,00,528)
  and profit breakdown
- **Interactive Power BI Dashboard** — Filters, KPI cards,
  and visual charts for dynamic data exploration
- **Business Insights Reporting** — Actionable findings
  including re-evaluation of Low Value segment which
  contributes 47.9% of total revenue
- **Profit Margin Tracking** — Overall margin of 12.8%
  with best-performing customer at 14.3% (Cust_1641)

## Key Insights

###  Customer Segmentation Overview
- Total of **6 customers** segmented into 3 groups:
  **High Value (2), Medium Value (1), Low Value (3)**
- Total revenue across all customers: **₹25,06,966**
- Total profit across all customers: **₹3,21,557**
- Overall profit margin: **12.8%**

###  High Value Customers
- **Cust_1088** is the top customer with **₹4,51,006 in sales**
  and **₹54,394 in profit**
- **Cust_1641** is the second-highest with **₹4,42,237 in sales**
  and the **highest profit of ₹63,342** among all customers
- Together, High Value customers contribute **₹8,93,243 (35.6%)**
  of total sales and **₹1,17,737 (36.6%)** of total profit
- Cust_1641 has a **14.3% profit margin** — the best among all customers

###  Medium Value Customers
- **Cust_26** is the sole Medium Value customer with
  **₹4,13,194 in sales** and **₹47,982 in profit**
- Profit margin of **11.6%** — slightly below average
- Contributes **16.5% of total sales** alone

###  Low Value Customers — Surprising Finding
- Despite being labeled "Low Value", this segment generates
  the **highest total sales of ₹12,00,528 (47.9% of total revenue)**
- Low Value customers contribute **₹1,55,837 in profit (48.5% of total)**
- **Cust_839** has a strong profit margin of **14.4%** —
  highest among Low Value customers
- The "Low Value" label likely reflects **purchase frequency or
  order size**, not total revenue contribution

###  Business Recommendations
- **Retain High Value customers** (Cust_1088, Cust_1641) with
  loyalty programs — they have the highest individual sales
- **Upsell to Cust_26 (Medium Value)** — sales potential exists
  to move them to High Value segment
- **Re-evaluate Low Value label** — this segment drives nearly
  50% of revenue and should not be deprioritized
- **Focus on Cust_1641** for repeat business —
  highest profit margin at 14.3%
